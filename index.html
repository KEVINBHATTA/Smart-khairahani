About Our Web App

This web application is designed for the citizens of Khairahani Municipality to report any issues related to public properties like roads, electricity, water lines, and health posts. Uploaded issues are verified through community voting and handled by ward and municipal offices. Together, we aim to improve transparency, efficiency, and communication between citizens and their local government.
